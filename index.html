Leonida Daily

An unofficial GTA 6 fan page. We post short clips, tips, hidden spots and launch news on YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

What we post

  • Recaps of official news from Rockstar Games, PlayStation and Xbox, with a link to the source.
  • How-to tips and hidden spots.
  • Gameplay clips from official trailers, from videos their creators released under a Creative Commons license, and from creators who have allowed us to share their work. Every clip credits its creator.

Our uploader app

We publish to our own YouTube channel and Instagram account with a small app we run ourselves. It only acts on our own accounts. It does not sign in other people or collect their data. See the privacy policy.

Takedowns and contact

If you made a clip we posted and want it removed, open an issue at github.com/bitflicker64/leonida-daily/issues. We remove it the same day, with no questions asked.
