Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Leonida Daily uploader, the app that publishes videos to the Leonida Daily YouTube channel and Instagram account.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of Leonida Daily. The app signs in to the page's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. No one else signs in to it, and it never asks other people for access to their accounts.

What data the app uses

  • YouTube (through the YouTube API Services): it uploads videos to our own channel and reads the view, like and comment counts of our own videos.
  • Instagram (through the Instagram Graph API): it publishes Reels to our own account and reads the counts and insights of our own posts.

The app does not read, collect or store personal data about viewers, commenters or any other users.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer. Post ids, links and view counts are kept in local files on that computer to track which posts work. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

How to revoke access

The owner can revoke the app's access to the YouTube channel at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and to Instagram from the Facebook and Instagram security settings. Removing access stops the app at once.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact
